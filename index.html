About the Foundation

Founded in 1997 through the visionary philanthropy of Gerry DeBlois, Vineyard Youth Tennis was built upon a simple promise: that every child living on Martha's Vineyard should have access to world-class athletic instruction at zero financial cost.

Located on Barnes Road in Oak Bluffs, the foundation operates four dedicated tennis courts, an educational clubhouse, and a tennis library designed to support academic study alongside physical training.

As a private operating charitable foundation, VYT receives no municipal or state tax dollars, sustained permanently by private endowment and committed community benefactors.

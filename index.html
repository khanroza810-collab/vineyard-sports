Fostering Sportsmanship, Discipline & Championship Youth Athletics

Founded on Martha's Vineyard in 1997 through the visionary philanthropy of Gerald DeBlois, Vineyard Youth Tennis has provided tuition-free instruction, Har-Tru red clay court training, and competitive tournament preparation to generations of island children.

The Origin & Vision of Vineyard Youth Tennis (1997–Present)

Vineyard Youth Tennis (VYT) was established in 1997 as a charitable foundation dedicated to removing economic barriers to sports participation. The program originated through the quiet, profound generosity of West Tisbury resident Gerald "Gerry" DeBlois, whose mission was uncompromising: to provide free tennis instruction and court access to every year-round child residing on Martha's Vineyard from first grade through high school.

What began as grassroots clinics inside local elementary school gymnasiums in Oak Bluffs and West Tisbury soon transitioned into a world-class training center. DeBlois acquired an 11-acre forested parcel of land off Barnes Road in Oak Bluffs, adjacent to the historic roundabout, funding the entire construction of a state-of-the-art tennis center that officially opened in July 2002.

Milestones of Our Community Journey

  • 1997
    Foundation Inception

    Gerald DeBlois establishes the Vineyard Youth Tennis Foundation, underwriting school-based clinics to make tennis a life sport for island youth.

  • 2002
    Grand Opening of Barnes Road Center

    The permanent facility opens in Oak Bluffs featuring four championship Har-Tru clay courts, a custom-designed clubhouse by Chuck Sullivan, and winter bubble dome enclosures.

  • 2006 – 2018
    Championship Golden Era

    Under the executive directorship of Scott Smith and head professional Michael Hallisky, VYT trains hundreds of juniors annually, fueling multiple Massachusetts High School Division State Championships for the MVRHS varsity teams.

  • 2019 – Present
    Expanded Athletics & Modern Community Sports

    Evolving to embrace community wellness, adult tennis clinics, pickleball integration, and multi-sport athletic fitness and matchday bulletins.

World-Class Har-Tru Clay Courts & Facilities

Unlike commercial municipal facilities that rely predominantly on hard courts, Vineyard Youth Tennis was purposefully designed with championship Har-Tru red clay courts. Gerald DeBlois insisted on clay because it encourages tactical rally development, reduces impact stress on young growing joints, and teaches nuanced footwork.

4 Har-Tru Clay Courts

Laser-leveled subterranean-irrigated clay courts that deliver authentic European-style sliding and consistent bounce dynamics.

Seasonal Air Dome (The Bubble)

Custom-engineered inflatable dome installed over courts each autumn, ensuring continuous, climate-controlled winter training.

Craftsman Clubhouse

Designed by island architect Chuck Sullivan and designer Twanette Tharp, featuring locker rooms, conference rooms, video analysis tech, and viewing decks.

Ball Machine & Training Alleys

Dedicated stroke-repetition stations, high-speed radar monitoring, and agility ladders for athletic conditioning.

Comprehensive Youth Development Programs

Our curriculum spans grassroots foundational mechanics up through nationally ranked junior tournament pathways:

Red & Orange Ball Grassroots (Ages 6–10)

Utilizing low-compression balls and scaled-down courts to cultivate fundamental hand-eye coordination, balance, and proper stroke mechanics in a high-energy, fun setting.

Green Ball & Junior Varsity Circuit (Ages 11–14)

Focusing on baseline consistency, approach shot transitions, topspin mechanics, and introductory match play strategies adhering to USTA junior standards.

High Performance & Tournament Squads (Ages 15–18)

Intensive tactical coaching, mental resilience training, point construction, and conditioning that directly prepared players for varsity dominance and collegiate tennis recruitment.

Multi-Sport Athletic Integration & Community Wellness

Athletic excellence is not confined to tennis. Over the past decade, Vineyard Youth Tennis expanded its athletic philosophy to encompass multi-sport physical literacy, providing conditioning support for football, soccer, and rugby athletes during their competitive seasons.

By blending cardiovascular stamina, lateral footwork drills, and core athletic stability, our programs help youth athletes excel across diverse sporting disciplines throughout Massachusetts and the wider New England athletic conference.
